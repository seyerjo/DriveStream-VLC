# 🤝 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **DriveStream VLC (`drive-vlc`)** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 2. Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
* 🌟 Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
* 🌟 Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
* 🌟 Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* 🌟 Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* ❌ The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* ❌ Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* ❌ Public or private harassment.
* ❌ Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.

## 3. Enforcement Responsibilities & Guidelines
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

In instances of reported violations, the project leadership will follow the enforcement guidelines to determine the consequences for the actions.

## 📧 Reporting & Contact
Project leaders will follow these standards in all community activities. Infractions can be reported directly to the project administrator via email.

> ⚠️ For explicit operational rules on how to submit code changes, feature branches, and pull requests under these standards, please consult the official **`CONTRIBUTING_GUIDELINES.md`** file located in the project root.
